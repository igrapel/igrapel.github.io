Ilan Grapel

I teach computer science, physics, and mathematics, have published machine learning research, and am a New York attorney.

I am based in Orlando, Florida, and teach AP Physics C at Hagerty High School. Before that I taught AP and IB Computer Science in Miami, oversaw information technology at Meoros High School, and built IB mathematics and physics video courses for Nail IB that reached about 13,000 students. I also spent three summers as a National Science Foundation research fellow at Florida International University, working on adversarial machine learning.

I came to teaching from law. I hold a J.D. from Emory, clerked at the Supreme Court of Israel, and researched transitional justice in Colombia and Northern Ireland. I still work in law: since 2025 I have been a legal researcher at The Heritage, LLC, covering corruption cases and international humanitarian and human rights law.

I am looking for work in technology education, computer science teaching, or legal technology. My resume has the details, and you can write to me directly.
